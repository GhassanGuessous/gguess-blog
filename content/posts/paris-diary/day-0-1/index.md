*Paris, 6 to 11 November 2026. This is the first part of my diary, written from my notes. Days 2 and onward are coming.*

## Day 0: Landing in Paris (Friday, 6 November)

### Marrakech → Orly

My flight left Marrakech (RAK) at 15:30 and landed at Paris Orly at 19:30. By the time I collected my bags it was already dark, and I was ready to get to the studio and rest.

### The metro with two suitcases

From Orly I took **line 14** to Châtelet, then **line 11** to **Pyrénées**, in the 20th arrondissement. It sounds simple on a map. With two suitcases, a warm jacket (Paris in November is not Marrakech) and a few staircases, it was a small workout.

### Hotel Le Bellevue, rue de Ménilmontant

From the station it was a walk down rue des Pyrénées to the hotel. By the time I got there I was sweating, dragging my luggage, and the code on the entrance door refused to cooperate. It worked on the **third try**.

Just as the door opened, two tenants were walking in. They saw me, my jacket and my two suitcases, and let me take the small elevator alone. A nice first bit of Parisian courtesy.

And then I was in my little studio. I called my mom to tell her I'd arrived safely, took a shower, and got ready for bed.

It was a Friday night and the café on the ground floor was full. People were staying out late and the street was lively. Any other night it might have bothered me, but I was so tired that I fell asleep immediately.

## Day 1: Eiffel Tower, Champs-Élysées and a Sparkling Finale (Saturday, 7 November)

### An early start

I woke up around 7 or 8 without an alarm, after sleeping really well. I got ready and went out. The sky was clear and sunny, just as the forecast had promised.

I walked down the street to **Gambetta** and took **line 3, then line 9**, heading to **Trocadéro**.

### First face-to-face with the Eiffel Tower

At around 9 am I saw the Eiffel Tower in person for the first time. The view wasn't perfectly clear, because the sun was rising right behind it, but that made it even more magical.

There were already people around, but it wasn't crowded yet. I took my time, looked at it from different angles, and took a *lot* of photos.

{{< figure src="eiffel-sunrise.webp" alt="The Eiffel Tower with the sun rising behind it" caption="First look. The sun was right behind it." >}}

### The first (and coolest) Parisian I met

While I was taking pictures, a middle-aged man came over and asked in English if I wanted him to take a photo of me with the monument. He had sensed that I was hoping for one.

I said, "Yes, and I'll take one of you after."

He smiled and replied, "I'm Parisian. I just like to enjoy this view sometimes."

After he took my photo, I switched to French and asked him: *"Avez-vous déjà visité le Maroc ?"*

*"Je connais très bien le Maroc,"* he said. *"Vous êtes de quelle ville ?"*

We talked about my country. He had visited cities I haven't seen yet, like **Fès** and **Meknès**, and of course **Marrakech**, my hometown. I wished him a good day, he wished me a good stay in Paris, and he went on his way.

He was the first Parisian I talked to and, honestly, still the coolest.

### Camoëns Avenue and the Seine

I headed to a viewpoint called **Camoëns Avenue**, where I took more shots. I photographed a couple, and they returned the favor.

Then I walked down to the **Pont d'Iéna** and enjoyed the Seine with the Tower beside it. I noticed a lot of **runners** along the river. I watched them with a bit of envy and wished I could join them. What a place to run.

I continued towards the Tower through the park below, along the **Champ de Mars**, and got a close look at the monument and all its details.

### Breakfast on rue Cler

By this point I still hadn't had breakfast. I headed to **rue Cler**, bought some fresh, warm *viennoiseries* from a boulangerie, and sat in a café for 30 to 45 minutes to rest.

### Avenue Montaigne to the Arc de Triomphe

Next was the **Pont de l'Alma**. From there I walked up the luxurious **Avenue Montaigne** to the **Champs-Élysées**, then in the direction of the **Arc de Triomphe**, stopping in a few shops and enjoying the stroll.

I took the **underground passage** to reach the Arc itself. What a monument. I photographed every corner and detail, then sat for a while to rest my legs.

For the way back I took the **other side of the Champs-Élysées**. When lunch time came I found a kebab place nearby. Not exactly haute cuisine, but it was just what I needed.

### Avenue George V and the lights

I walked back to the Alma along the equally luxurious **Avenue George V**. By then it was already dark. (November days in Paris are short!)

From the bridge I stopped for a moment to enjoy the **illuminated Eiffel Tower**. Then I went to **rue de l'Université** for a close view and took photos of the Tower **sparkling at 19:00**.

{{< figure src="eiffel-sparkle.jpg" alt="The Eiffel Tower sparkling at night" caption="7 pm on rue de l'Université." >}}

### Back home

I finished the day by walking back to Alma metro station and taking **line 9, then line 3** back to Gambetta, tired and happy.
